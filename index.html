About

I work as a Data Scientist at VALUNY (formerly ITSC GmbH). I want AI to support the people who already do the work in health insurance, not to replace them.

Before moving into industry, I spent five years as a researcher at the Heinz Nixdorf Institute. There I earned my Ph.D. in theoretical computer science and published 13 peer-reviewed papers on distributed algorithms and robot coordination. That background shapes how I break down complex problems, and I use it every day when I build ML pipelines and RAG-based systems.
